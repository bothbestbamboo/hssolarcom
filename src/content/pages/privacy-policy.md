Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Homesun Solar Technology Co., Ltd. collects, uses, and safeguards the information you provide when browsing our website or submitting an inquiry.

## Information Collection and Use

Homesun Solar Technology Co., Ltd. is the sole owner of the information collected on this site. We only collect information that you voluntarily provide via inquiry forms, email, or direct communication. We never sell, rent, or share your personal or business information with unauthorized third parties outside our organization.

We use your information strictly to respond to your inquiries, prepare technical proposals, and process orders. After an order is confirmed, we may request your shipping address and contact phone number to prepare export and logistics documentation.

Order records—including order date, contact name, product specifications, shipping address, payment reference, shipment date, and tracking number—are stored securely so our team can provide accurate after-sales and warranty support. For private-label and OEM/ODM partners, we enforce strict confidentiality over all project and client details.

Unless you opt out, we may occasionally send email updates regarding new solar product releases, technical datasheets, or changes to this policy.

## Your Access to and Control Over Information

You may opt out of future communications or manage your stored data at any time by contacting us via email or phone:

- Request to view what information we hold about you, if any.
- Update or correct any inaccurate contact or company details.
- Request deletion of your contact records from our system.
- Raise any questions or concerns regarding how your data is handled.

## Data Security

We implement both online and offline safeguards to protect your information. Whenever you submit an inquiry through our website, data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Offline, access to customer records is restricted solely to authorized staff who require the information to fulfill quotes, billing, logistics, or customer support.
